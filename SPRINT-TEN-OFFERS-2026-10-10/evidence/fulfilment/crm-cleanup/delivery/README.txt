CRM Duplicate and Data-Quality Check: what is in this folder
Sample made from invented data.

report.html
  The whole report. Double-click it to open it in any web browser. To keep a PDF copy, choose Print in the browser, then Save as PDF.

01-duplicate-groups.csv
  Duplicate groups: the table as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

02-data-quality-issues.csv
  Data-quality issues: the table as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

03-proposed-changes.csv
  Proposed changes (you review and apply them in your CRM): the table as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

04-stale-leads.csv
  Stale leads: the list as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

Every message in this report is a draft for you to review, edit and send yourself. Nothing was sent to anyone, and nothing in your systems was changed.
A person reviews this report before it is sent to you.
