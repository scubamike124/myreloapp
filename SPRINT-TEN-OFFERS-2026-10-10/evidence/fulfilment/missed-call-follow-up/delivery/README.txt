Missed-Call and Web-Inquiry Follow-Up List: what is in this folder
Sample made from invented data.

report.html
  The whole report. Double-click it to open it in any web browser. To keep a PDF copy, choose Print in the browser, then Save as PDF.

01-open-leads-most-urgent-first.csv
  Open leads, most urgent first: the table as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

02-already-followed-up.csv
  Already followed up: the table as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

03-missed-calls-by-time-of-day.csv
  Missed calls by time of day: the table as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

04-draft-replies-for-open-leads.csv
  Draft replies for open leads: the draft messages as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

05-call-back-by-a-person-now.csv
  Call back by a person now (no written draft): the list as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

Every message in this report is a draft for you to review, edit and send yourself. Nothing was sent to anyone, and nothing in your systems was changed.
A person reviews this report before it is sent to you.
