Unpaid Invoice Tracker with Draft Reminders: what is in this folder
Sample made from invented data.

report.html
  The whole report. Double-click it to open it in any web browser. To keep a PDF copy, choose Print in the browser, then Save as PDF.

01-aging.csv
  Aging: the table as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

02-outstanding-by-customer.csv
  Outstanding by customer: the table as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

03-reminders-due-this-week.csv
  Reminders due this week: the table as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

04-draft-reminders.csv
  Draft reminders: the draft messages as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

05-not-chased-and-why.csv
  Not chased, and why: the list as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

Every message in this report is a draft for you to review, edit and send yourself. Nothing was sent to anyone, and nothing in your systems was changed.
A person reviews this report before it is sent to you.
