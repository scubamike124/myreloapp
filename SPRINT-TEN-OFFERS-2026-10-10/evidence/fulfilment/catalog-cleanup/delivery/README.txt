Product Catalog Cleanup and Cross-Store Check: what is in this folder
Sample made from invented data.

report.html
  The whole report. Double-click it to open it in any web browser. To keep a PDF copy, choose Print in the browser, then Save as PDF.

01-issues-by-product.csv
  Issues by product: the table as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

02-cross-store-differences.csv
  Cross-store differences: the table as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

03-proposed-changes.csv
  Proposed changes (you review and import them yourself): the table as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

04-skus-in-one-store-only.csv
  SKUs in one store only: the list as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

Every message in this report is a draft for you to review, edit and send yourself. Nothing was sent to anyone, and nothing in your systems was changed.
A person reviews this report before it is sent to you.
