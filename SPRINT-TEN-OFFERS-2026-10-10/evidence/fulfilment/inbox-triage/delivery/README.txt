Support Inbox Triage with Reply Drafts: what is in this folder
Sample made from invented data.

report.html
  The whole report. Double-click it to open it in any web browser. To keep a PDF copy, choose Print in the browser, then Save as PDF.

01-triage.csv
  Triage: the table as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

02-draft-replies.csv
  Draft replies: the draft messages as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

03-needs-a-person.csv
  Needs a person (no draft written): the list as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

04-what-customers-wrote-about-most.csv
  What customers wrote about most: the list as a spreadsheet file. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

Every message in this report is a draft for you to review, edit and send yourself. Nothing was sent to anyone, and nothing in your systems was changed.
A person reviews this report before it is sent to you.
