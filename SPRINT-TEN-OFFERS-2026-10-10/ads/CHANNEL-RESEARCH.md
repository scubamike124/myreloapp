# Channel research: where Amber One AI may promote its pilot offers for free

Researched 2026-10-11 for the owner's directive "expand the free promotion work beyond Facebook: research LinkedIn, Pinterest, Nextdoor and additional relevant sites where businesses are allowed to promote services and link to our landing pages; verify each site's current posting rules, eligibility, free limits and supported automation before integrating it." Research only: no account was created, no sign-up flow opened, nothing posted, nobody contacted.

## How far to trust this (read first)

- **No platform page was opened.** The sandbox cannot reach any outside website (every fetch fails at DNS), so every fact below reached the researchers as a **search-result summary**. "Official" means the search tool attributed the text or number to an official page, not that anyone read the page. Before relying on exact wording in a policy-sensitive step, re-read the live page. Each row says how sure it is.
- **The search budget ran out.** The turn's shared limit of 200 searches was used up, so some platforms have **no research at all**: they are marked *not researched* and their verdicts are provisional leads from background knowledge. The exact searches still needed are listed at the end.
- **Tags.** OFFICIAL = attributed to the platform's own page. SECONDARY = a blog, news item, forum or vendor. INFERRED = reasoning from the facts next to it. BACKGROUND = memory, unchecked. Dates are given only where the result showed one.
- **Verdict words.** INTEGRATE: nothing but account creation by the owner and a credential the owner makes in their own settings (no payment, no review). INTEGRATE-AFTER-OWNER-STEP: one more named owner-only gate first (a developer app, tester consent, an approval). MANUAL-ONLY: allowed, but no free write API for us, or the API breaks the no-spend rule; a person posts by hand. NOT-RELEVANT / NOT-ALLOWED: do not use.
- **What the owner's rules rule out everywhere:** paid plans or ad spend, Reddit, unsolicited DMs or cold email, scripted sign-ups or browser bots, engagement bait, anything that bypasses an account, identity, CAPTCHA or anti-spam requirement. The owner-only steps on every platform are creating the account, solving any CAPTCHA, verifying phone or email where the platform asks for the person, and clicking through any consent screen.

## What was built, and in what order

Order by cost to the owner and value (inferred): **our own tip pages** (nothing to do), **Bluesky** (an account and an app password), **Threads** (a Meta app use case, a tester role, Connect), **LinkedIn profile** (a developer app, Connect, a `promo-page:` line), then **Facebook** (the Meta login that has been outstanding since August, now with a 2-link-a-month cap). Pinterest needs a weeks-long approval; Nextdoor has no confirmed API; X is paid; Google Business, Instagram and the rest give no per-offer link or are not eligible.

The lane (`src/lib/offers/promotion.ts`) holds each channel to its own weekday slot, its own weekly count, Facebook's monthly count, and a 72-hour gap before the same offer is posted again on a channel. The exact owner steps per channel, with prefilled profile text, are in `ads/OWNER-STEPS.md`.

## Facts that matter and what changed recently

- **Meta One (2026-09-15)** changed free link promotion on Meta platforms: some free Facebook Pages are limited to 2 organic link posts (or comments) per month; clickable links in Instagram posts are a paid-tier feature. Reported per-tier numbers conflict (one source: Advanced 8, Expert 20; another: only the top tier removes the cap; another: Advanced allows 4). Whether an API post counts toward the cap is unknown.
- **LinkedIn's `Linkedin-Version` header** must be a version the Posts API page still lists: 202508 sunset 2026-08-17 and **202510 sunsets 2026-10-15**. The code holds one constant to change (`LINKEDIN_VERSION`).
- **Pinterest** changes its Terms of Service and Community Guidelines effective **2026-11-12**; re-check then.
- **X** pay-per-use pricing (changelog 2026-04-16): $0.015 per post, $0.20 per post with a URL. Sources disagree on any new-signup credit.
- **Google Business Profile** policy change reported 2026-10-06 (no phone, email, social or unrelated links in posts); API application backlog announced 2026-09-15.
- **Threads** `link_attachment` is for text-only posts. A Threads login lasts about an hour as it comes from the login code; Amber extends it to a long-lived token (60 days) and extends it again before it expires (`th_exchange_token`, `th_refresh_token`, from memory of Meta's documentation).
- **Bluesky** allows one embed type per post, so a post has a link card (with the sample image as its thumbnail) or images, not both; a link facet carries the full address.
