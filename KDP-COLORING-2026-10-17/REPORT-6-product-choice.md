## 2. Secondary sources (not verified against Amazon; treat as colour)

- "Cozy" and "bold and easy" are described as a documented trend; one blog counts close to half of Amazon's top adult coloring titles in October 2025 as bold-and-easy; titles such as *Cozy Corners* (Nov 2025) and *Cozy Nooks* (Apr 2026, retro rooms and reading nooks) exist, so "nook" titles are taken.
- Vendors advise publishing Christmas titles 6 to 12 weeks before the peak (so mid-October is late), call a plain "Christmas coloring book" saturated, recommend narrower or winter-wide titles that keep selling after Christmas, and say single-use holiday books sell for about six to eight weeks a year.
- KDP paperback review is reported at about 72 hours, then up to about 72 hours more to appear in search (blogs quoting KDP; KDP's own help page gives no figure).

## 6. What I do not know, and the risks

- **Art quality is the main risk.** The leaders' charm is illustration; scenes drawn from vector shapes will look simpler. The six-scene sample exists to let you judge that before the book is built.
- Late for the Christmas peak by the vendors' own rule; the winter framing extends the tail to February.
- A book with no reviews sells slowly without ads (not allowed without your approval); its first visibility is the New Releases list and search.
- No sales estimate: I cannot rank products by copies from this snapshot, and I will not guess.
- The keywords I would use are untested guesses; no search-volume data was collected.
