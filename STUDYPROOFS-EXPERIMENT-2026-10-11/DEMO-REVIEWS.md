# StudyProofs demonstrations: what was made, what was checked, what was decided

The rule (owner, 2026-10-11): a demonstration is made by the product's own pipeline from sample notes we wrote, shows the input, the study tools and where to try them, and is not edited. **One exception, made on the owner's instruction of 2026-10-12 ("Correct the ambiguous biology question"):** BIO 101 question 3 was reworded by a person, and the example page says so and shows the product's original under it (see below). Before any is used, a person (here: me, reading the data against the notes) checks it. A study set that cites a line which is not in the notes is not used at all.

How a demonstration is made: the worker runs the product's pipeline (`demo-gen.ts`, pipeline version 2) over a sample note, up to five times, and keeps the first set that passes the product's own bar (enough cards and questions, and every citation a real line of the notes); it posts the data as a comment on #105 (marker `amber-studyproofs-demo:<slug>`). The files here are made from those comments by `tools/demo-from-comment.mjs`, `tools/render-demo.mjs` and `tools/make-kit.mjs`.

## Update, 2026-10-13 (overnight): why HIST 120 keeps failing, and the draft fix

The failures are the same each round: one or two cards or questions **with no citation**. The cause is in the product's generation step, not in the notes: the first model call is asked for a `sourceQuote` with every item, the model paraphrases some, the product's check (`verifyQuote`) rightly rejects a paraphrase, and the item is kept with no source line. On a student's own upload the same thing shows as a card without a source. **Draft amberai pull request #817** (branch `claude/sp-citation-repair`; not merged, not deployed) adds a second, narrow model call that shows the model only the uncited cards and questions, with the same excerpts, and asks for nothing but the exact words each came from; a quote that verifies is attached, one that does not leaves the item uncited. It never adds, removes or rewrites an item, so a set whose items were all cited is unchanged (BIO 101 and STAT 200 stay as reviewed, and the pipeline version stays 2). ## BIO 101: Cellular respiration

- All eight flashcards agree with the notes (the overall equation; glycolysis in the cytoplasm; pyruvate oxidation gives 1 CO2 and 1 NADH per pyruvate; the Krebs cycle gives 2 ATP per glucose; oxygen is the final electron acceptor; fermentation regenerates NAD+ so glycolysis can go on; most ATP comes from the electron transport chain; 6 CO2 per glucose in total).
- Quiz: question 1 (net ATP from glycolysis: 2), 2 (what the Krebs cycle produces per glucose), 4 (lactic acid fermentation in muscle cells) are right and clear. **Question 3 is a weak question:** "What is the primary function of the electron transport chain?" has the choices "To produce ATP", "To oxidize NADH", "To pump H+ ions", "To produce CO2", and the key is "To pump H+ ions". The key and the explanation agree with the line it cites (the notes say the chain pumps H+ and builds a gradient, and that ATP synthase makes most of the ATP), but a student could reasonably pick "To produce ATP" or "To oxidize NADH". When this was first written it was kept as the product made it (editing it would have broken "unedited"); the owner then asked for it to be corrected, and the decision below replaced it. It was never used in the video, the feed image or the pin (the renderer selects the first eligible question, which is question 1), and the example page shows it with its source line next to the line "AI can make mistakes, so check any study tool against your own notes".
- **Decision, 2026-10-12 (owner: "Correct the ambiguous biology question"):** question 3 is reworded, not removed. New wording, asking only what the cited line says: "According to the notes, what does the energy from the electrons do in the electron transport chain?", choices "It splits glucose into two pyruvate", "It turns pyruvate into lactate", **"It pumps H+ across the membrane, building a proton gradient"** (the key), "It releases CO2"; the explanation, topic and cited line are the product's. The product's own wording is kept in the example's `edits` (amberai branch `claude/sp-examples-first-two`) and the page says "One change by a person", marks the question "Reworded by a person on 2026-10-12" and shows the original under it with its answer key.
