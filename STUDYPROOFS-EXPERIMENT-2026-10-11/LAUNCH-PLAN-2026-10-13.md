## 1. The product, stated accurately (the only claims any post may make)

- **What it does:** paste notes, or upload a text PDF, and get flashcards, a quiz and study notes back; where it can, each item shows the line of your notes it came from, and it says when it cannot find one. Tutor answers point back to a page or quote in your upload; if it is not in your file, it says so instead of guessing.
- **Free plan:** 3 uploads a week; 12 AI generations a month; up to 40 cards and one quiz of up to 8 questions per upload; 10 cited tutor answers a day. What you make stays in your library: AI limits only stop new generation, and cards you already made stay after you cancel.
- **Paid plans:** Student $4.99 a month or $39 a year; Pro $9.99 a month or $79 a year (practice exams, "practice only, never predicted questions"). Audio is metered (Student 60 minutes a month, Pro 5 hours): "not unlimited recording". Cancel in Stripe's customer portal.
- **Limits worth saying out loud:** no OCR, so a scanned or copy-protected PDF yields no text and the product says so; AI can make mistakes (every post carries that line); it is a young product.
- **Never say:** unlimited recording, predicted exam questions, guaranteed grades, "cheaper than" or "alternative to" a named product, testimonials or results we do not have, or that every card from a student's own notes is cited (true for the two samples, not promised for anyone's notes).
- **The two approved examples (the only ones used):** BIO 101 "Cellular respiration" (8 flashcards, 4 quiz questions, 7 note sections; 19 of 19 quotes are in the notes; quiz question 3 was reworded by a person on 2026-10-12 and the page says so and shows the product's original) and STAT 200 "Hypothesis testing basics" (8 flashcards, 8 quiz questions, 6 note sections; 22 of 22 quotes are in the notes; exactly as the product made it). Files: `demos/cell-respiration/` and `demos/hypothesis-testing/` (a 32-second silent vertical video with captions, a feed image, a pin image, a preview image each). Review record: `DEMO-REVIEWS.md`.

### Quora (a complete answer first; the mention last, disclosed)

- **Answer 1, to a question like "What is the best way to turn lecture notes into flashcards?" (day 5):** Make the cards from your own notes, not from a textbook summary: one idea per card, a question on the front and the shortest true answer on the back; keep the line of the notes it came from next to the card so you can check it later; mix topics when you review instead of going through one chapter at a time; and test yourself before you reread. If you use a tool to make the cards, pick one that shows where each card came from and tells you when it could not find a source. Disclosure: I make StudyProofs, which does this from pasted notes or a text PDF and shows the line each card came from where it can; the free plan is 3 uploads a week; it can make mistakes, so check the cards against your notes: https://hq.amberoneai.com/studyproofs?src=quora&campaign=answer-1
- **Answer 2, to a question like "Are AI-generated flashcards accurate?" (day 9):** Not by default. An AI tool can write a card that sounds right and is not in your material at all. Three checks: ask for the exact line each card came from and reject cards without one; spot-check five cards against the source before you trust the set; and never study a card whose answer you cannot find in your own notes. Disclosure: I make StudyProofs, built around exactly that check (each card shows its source line where it can find one, and says when it cannot); it still makes mistakes sometimes, which is why it shows its work: https://hq.amberoneai.com/studyproofs?src=quora&campaign=answer-2

Synthetic and test accounts are left out. No IP address or cookie is kept.
- **The post ledger.** For each post, one line in `LAUNCH-LEDGER.md` (to be created on day 1): date, platform, post URL, `src`, `campaign`, who posted. Amber adds each source's numbers from the tracker on day 7 and day 14, so every post is matched to its row.
- **How to read it.** Visitors but no signups: the page or the offer. Signups but no first set: the first upload step. First sets but no return: the product's value. Returns but no checkout: price or trust. For two weeks and a small audience, a working rule: a source with 10 or more signups, half of them making a first set and a fifth coming back, is kept and gets the second week's posts; a source with 50 or more visitors and no signup is stopped. A student who pays on day 12 has not shown renewal; the question the test can answer is use and return, not revenue.
- **What it cannot see.** A bio link carries the platform but not the post (counted under `tiktok-bio` and the like); a student who clicks on a phone and signs up on a laptop shows as direct; nothing identifies a person. If the direct count grows, the one simple addition is an optional "how did you hear about us" line at signup; that is a code change for your approval, not built.

## 7. What is not done, and what is yours

- **No account exists** on any platform in section 2; creating one is a signup and is yours, after you approve the plan.
- **The gate** is shut until the support address receives mail (the one DNS record for amberoneai.com, or an existing mailbox on another domain, as reported separately today); then the probe's walk passes and the tracker's verdict flips by itself.
- **#809** (the example pages for BIO 101 and STAT 200) is merged only with your approval, after the gate opens.
- **Reddit** stays out unless you lift the rule; its rules could not be read from here in any case. **Hacker News** would need your own words and a try-without-signup page.
- **Nothing was contacted:** tutoring centers, creators, moderators and newsletters are outside this plan.
