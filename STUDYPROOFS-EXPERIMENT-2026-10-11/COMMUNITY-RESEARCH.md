### 5.2 Demo-video checklist (applies to TikTok, Reels, Shorts and video pins)

1. **Show the real product on invented or public-domain/openly licensed notes.** No real student's notes, no grades, no testimonials (owner rule).
2. **None of the forbidden claims** (unlimited lecture recording, predicted exam questions, guaranteed grades/GPA, "cheaper Turbo").
3. **Founder disclosure on screen and in the caption:** "Made by the StudyProofs founder." On TikTok also switch on **Disclose commercial content, then "Your brand"**, which adds a label to the post [O: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-turn-on-the-commercial-content-disclosure-setting-in-tiktok; the search could not confirm the toggle wording in the live app]. One vendor says undisclosed content can be pulled from For You within 24 hours [S: https://www.bazaarvoice.com/updates/the-new-rules-of-tiktok-what-brands-and-creators-need-to-know/].
4. **AI honesty line:** "Cards are AI-generated and can contain mistakes; check them." Label realistic AI footage or voices: TikTok requires visible labelling of realistic synthetic media [S, 2023 coverage, current text [NC]: https://petapixel.com/2023/03/21/tiktoks-new-guidelines-say-ai-generated-media-must-be-disclosed]; YouTube requires the "Altered content" disclosure for realistic altered or synthetic content, and does **not** require it for AI used for scripts, ideas or captions or for clearly unrealistic/animated content [O: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491]. A plain screen recording needs neither.
5. **Export a clean file; no other app's watermark.** Instagram has said it does not recommend Reels carrying other apps' logos; a creator's own logo is fine [S: https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-including-your-own-logo-on-a-reel-is-ok/730852, https://tubefilter.com/2021/02/10/instagram-reels-with-tiktok-watermark-less-discoverable/ (2021)].
6. **Music:** a TikTok Business account is limited to the Commercial Music Library, and unlicensed music on business accounts can be muted or removed; Instagram business accounts are reportedly similar (Instagram specifics [NC]) [S: https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/why-can-i-only-use-commercial-sounds-on-tiktok, https://planoly.com/blog/commercially-licensed-vs-trending-sounds-what-can-brands-use, https://hellothematic.com/music-rules-tiktok-instagram-youtube/]. Safest: no music, or a library sound that stays inside the platform it was picked on.
7. **Do not rely on a caption link.** YouTube made links in Shorts descriptions and comments non-clickable on 2023-08-31 and sources still describe it that way in 2026 [S: https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=175414, https://app.unilink.us/blog/how-to-add-link-youtube-shorts]; Instagram captions are not clickable (CHANNEL-RESEARCH.md); TikTok video descriptions: CHANNELS.md says none, this research found nothing on it [NC]. Use the profile link (TikTok: 1,000 followers or a Registered Business Account [S]; Instagram bio link; YouTube channel About link [S]), and on Instagram the Story link sticker [S].
8. **Vertical 9:16, short.** Keep under 60 seconds [B, not searched].
