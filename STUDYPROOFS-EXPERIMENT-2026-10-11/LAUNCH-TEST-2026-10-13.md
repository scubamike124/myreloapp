Every post carries the line "Made by StudyProofs. AI can make mistakes, so check the cards against your notes." Nothing is a testimonial, a grade or a result; nothing says unlimited recording, predicted questions, guaranteed grades, or compares with another product. Until #809 is deployed, replace each example-page link with `https://hq.amberoneai.com/studyproofs?src=...` and drop the words "here is a sample".

Rules that apply, from the platforms' own pages (details and sources in `LAUNCH-PLAN-2026-10-13.md`, section 2 and the appendix): YouTube's spam policy covers descriptions and external links and forbids flooding with repetitive variations; AI disclosure is only for realistic altered content; links in a Short's description are not clickable, so YouTube's job here is reach, and the channel's About page keeps its own link. LinkedIn forbids inappropriately commercial or repetitive content and requires your real identity; a post on your own profile is fine. X forbids bulk, duplicate or irrelevant posting and excessive unrelated hashtags. Meta's spam policy allows clearly identified branded content and cross-promotion that is not paid.

## 4. What is measured, and what decides

- **The tracker** (amberai #105, marker `amber-studyproofs-tracker`) already counts, per source: visitors, signups, first study set (the student made material), came back (the student used it again on a later day), free trials, checkouts, verified live payments. Sources in this test: `youtube`, `linkedin`, `x`, `facebook`, `examples` (the pages' own try-it links), and `discord` if used. It also lists referring domains, which catches a YouTube viewer who types the address rather than clicking.
- **The ledger:** one line per post in `LAUNCH-LEDGER.md` (created on day 1): date, platform, post URL, `src`, `campaign`. Amber fills in each source's numbers on day 7.
- **The reading on day 7.** Any source with 5 or more signups where at least half made a first study set: run that channel's part of the larger plan next. Visitors but no signups: the page or the offer, not the channel. Signups but no first set: the first upload step, and the next thing to fix before more promotion. No visit from any source after eight posts: the test says the existing accounts do not reach students, and the larger plan's new-account channels (TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest) are the next question for you.
- **Cost:** $0. Nothing is boosted, no listing is bought, no tool is subscribed to.
